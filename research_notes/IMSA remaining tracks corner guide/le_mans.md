# Circuit des 24 Heures du Mans (Circuit de la Sarthe) - corner and track reference

Research depth: ~11 tool calls. Several pages were read through a summarising fetch tool, so exact wording is not guaranteed. No source found gives per-corner distances from start/finish, an official corner numbering, or sector line positions.

## What are the correct names and order of all corners/sections on the current layout?

### Takeaway
Lap order (from start/finish): Pit straight, Dunlop Curve, Dunlop Chicane, Forest Esses, Tertre Rouge, Mulsanne Straight with two chicanes (Hunaudieres), Mulsanne Corner, Indianapolis, Arnage, Porsche Curves (with Maison Blanche), Karting Esses, Ford Chicanes, back to the pit straight. The Mulsanne chicanes date from 1990, NOT 2018 (the premise in the brief is wrong).

### Cited Findings
- Current layout (in use since 2018) is 13.626 km, 38 turns, race lap record 3:17.297 (Conway, Toyota, 2019). Lap length history: 13.629 km 2007-2017, 13.626 km 2018-present. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe)
- Two roughly equally spaced chicanes were added to the Mulsanne Straight before the 1990 race (FIA would not sanction a straight over 2 km). The 2018 change per Wikipedia was a re-profile of Tertre Rouge (radius moved ~200 m after a 2013 fatal crash) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe). (Summariser wording; verify the 2018 detail.) iRacing page also says two medium-speed chicanes added in 1990 — [iRacing](https://www.iracing.com/tracks/circuit-de-la-sarthe/)
- Dunlop chicane: added 1987 (entry speed ~290 to ~180 km/h), tightened 1994 and again in the 2006 redevelopment — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe)
- Ford chicanes: first dates to the late 1960s (Ford-Ferrari rivalry), second added early 1970s; they feed the pit straight — [ACO](https://24h-lemans.com/en/news/24-hours-of-le-mans-the-legendary-spots-on-the-circuit-de-la-sarthe-19372). Wikipedia says the Ford chicane was added for 1968.
- ACO named spots in order: Pit Straight, Dunlop Curve (named for Dunlop Bridge), Forest Esses (1930s, descending), Tertre Rouge (right-hander), Mulsanne Straight (Ligne Droite des Hunaudieres), Mulsanne Corner (right-hand kink, since 1986), Indianapolis (S-bend), Arnage (slowest part, right-angled), Porsche Curves (near flat out), Maison Blanche (near-straight, 1970s layout), Ford Corner (two chicanes) — [ACO](https://24h-lemans.com/en/news/24-hours-of-le-mans-the-legendary-spots-on-the-circuit-de-la-sarthe-19372)
- Pit de Brief lap order: Dunlop Curve, Dunlop Chicane, Forest Esses, Tertre Rouge, Mulsanne Straight, "Daytona Chicane", "Michelin Chicane", Mulsanne Corner, Indianapolis, Arnage, Porsche Curves, Karting Esses, Ford Chicanes, Motul Turn (final complex before pit straight) — [Pit de Brief](https://www.pitdebrief.com/?p=122722). Chicane names Daytona/Michelin and Motul Turn appear in this one source only; Motul Turn not verified elsewhere, and which chicane is which of the first/second is implied by order only.
- Maison Blanche and a "Corvette" chicane come before the Ford chicane (driver narrative, no details) — [Motor Sport](https://motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-2022/78/le-mans-circuit-lap-guide)

## Where are the pit entry and exit, and what is the pit lane speed limit?

### Takeaway
Pit limit is 60 km/h (2026 briefing notes). Pit entry is at the Ford chicane (before the pit straight); pit exit is at/near the Dunlop Curve. Pit lane length and exact positions are not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Max speed in pit lane 60 km/h per 2026 24 Hours of Le Mans briefing notes (via search snippet; PDF itself not opened) — [FIA WEC briefing notes](https://fiawec.alkamelsystems.com/Results_NoticeBoard/14_2026/03_24%20Hours%20of%20Le%20Mans/006_Doc%206%20-%20BRIEFING%20NOTES.pdf)
- Ford chicane braking references include the pit entry road (pit lane begins on the right, in driver terms) — [RaceControl](https://news.racecontrol.gg/tracks/le-mans-ultimate-circuit-de-la-sarthe-le-mans-track-guide/), [Motor Sport](https://motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-2022/78/le-mans-circuit-lap-guide)
- Pit exit white line is referenced for turn-in to Dunlop Curve (T1) — [RaceControl](https://news.racecontrol.gg/tracks/le-mans-ultimate-circuit-de-la-sarthe-le-mans-track-guide/)
- A 2006 test-day alternate pit lane was 450 m with a 60 km/h limit and rejoined beyond the Dunlop Chicane before the Dunlop Bridge. This is NOT the current pit lane — [Wikipedia 2006 24 Hours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans)

### Inferences
- Pit entry runs off the exit of the last Ford chicane onto the pit straight; exit joins at Dunlop Curve, so the pit lane spans the start/finish line (consistent with sources, not explicitly stated).

### Gaps
- Current pit lane length, exact entry/exit positions, number of boxes: not found.
- Pit lane speed limit during Full Course Yellow / slow zones: not researched.

## Main overtaking places and hard-to-pass sections

### Takeaway
Mulsanne Straight/chicanes is the main passing zone; GT3 traffic is the main hazard because it brakes latest.

### Cited Findings
- Mulsanne treated as the easiest place to pass slower cars; speed gap to GT cars usually helps Hypercar/LMP2; LMGT3 cars with ABS brake latest so faster classes must brake earlier; "Hypercar coming" calls to LMP2 roughly every five minutes (2023) — [Car Magazine](https://carmagazine.co.uk/features/car-culture/le-mans-2024-traffic)
- Driver advice: pass slower cars at the first Mulsanne chicane (100 m board); pass traffic before Arnage; pass round the outside of the first Porsche Curves part "when brave" — [Motor Sport](https://motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-2022/78/le-mans-circuit-lap-guide)
- Dunlop Chicane contact between LMP2 and GT3 in 2025; track is dirty offline — [Car Magazine](https://carmagazine.co.uk/features/car-culture/le-mans-2024-traffic), [a-e.li](https://a-e.li/news/motorsport/wec/2025-le-mans-24h-2-hours-highlights/)

### Inferences
- Hard to pass: Forest Esses, Porsche Curves (barriers close, flat-out), Arnage exit. Mulsanne chicane exits and Indianapolis are common class-passing points. Inference from the above, not a stated list.

### Gaps
- No IMSA-specific (GTP/LMP2/GTD) passing guidance; IMSA runs Le Mans only via the WEC/ACO event if at all, not verified.

## Elevation and surface notes

### Cited Findings
- Forest Esses descend; Indianapolis has banking (banked left); Dunlop Bridge spans the Dunlop Curve; big banking at the base of the "bowl" on the Esses; bump in the right-hander after the left sweeper — [ACO](https://24h-lemans.com/en/news/24-hours-of-le-mans-the-legendary-spots-on-the-circuit-de-la-sarthe-19372), [Pit de Brief](https://www.pitdebrief.com/?p=122722), [Motor Sport](https://motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-2022/78/le-mans-circuit-lap-guide)
- Surface: tarmac. Much of the circuit is public road (Mulsanne Straight is the D338) used by the race, not verified in these sources.
- Hypercars reach up to ~345 km/h on the straight — [Pit de Brief](https://www.pitdebrief.com/?p=122722)

## iRacing version

### Takeaway
iRacing's track is "Circuit des 24 Heures du Mans", with two configurations: "24 Heures du Mans" (8.47 mi / 13.63 km) and "Historic" (8.43 mi / 13.56 km). No published iRacing corner list or numbering was found.

### Cited Findings
- Configs and lengths — [iRacing](https://www.iracing.com/tracks/circuit-de-la-sarthe/) (page text); [simracing.wiki](https://simracing.wiki/Circuit_des_24_Heures_du_Mans_(iRacing))
- iRacing's description names Dunlop Curve/Bridge, Tertre Rouge, Mulsanne, Indianapolis, Arnage, Porsche Curves, Ford Chicanes and mentions real-world chicane history. It does not state a corner count or numbering, nor differences from reality — [iRacing](https://www.iracing.com/tracks/circuit-de-la-sarthe/), [simracing.wiki](https://simracing.wiki/Circuit_des_24_Heures_du_Mans_(iRacing))
- 13.63 km (24 Heures config) vs 13.626 km real: consistent with the current layout, so it is the 24 Heures config that matches the real 2018+ track. Historic is a shorter older layout (details not found).

### Inferences
- The "24 Heures du Mans" config is the one for IMSA/WEC-style racing. Because no numbering is published, the app should call corners by name.
