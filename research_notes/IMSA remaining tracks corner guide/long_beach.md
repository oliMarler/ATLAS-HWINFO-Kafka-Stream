# Long Beach Street Circuit - corner guide (IMSA / IndyCar current layout, iRacing)

Coverage note: sources found were thin on a per-corner basis. Several pages returned 403/429 (imsa.com Beach Drive, racer.com 2015 passing piece, overtake.gg LMU guide, IMSA hairpin article); their content below is only from search-result snippets and is marked "snippet only". No official gplb.com or IMSA turn-by-turn guide was retrieved.

## Corner list (current 11-turn layout, lap order from start/finish)

### Takeaway
Only T1, T2-3 (fountain complex), T4, T5, T6, T8, T9, T10 and T11 (hairpin) have any sourced description. No source gives official corner names beyond "hairpin" and the "dolphin fountain section", no source gives per-corner distances, and no source states T1-T8 left/right explicitly. Sourced directions: T9 right, T10 left, T11 right (hairpin). The user's telemetry (T1 left, T2-T5 right, T6-T7 left, T8-T9 right, T10 left, T11 right) is NOT contradicted by anything found, but T1-T8 directions are unconfirmed by sources.

### Cited Findings
- Circuit is 1.968 mi (3.167 km) with 11 turns, tight 90-degree corners, concrete walls and a signature hairpin — [RaceSchool](https://www.raceschool.com/tracks/long-beach-street-circuit/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Prix_of_Long_Beach)
- Hairpin is Turn 11 on the current layout, the slowest corner of the season at about 30 mph, "slower than pit lane speed"; its exit propels cars onto Shoreline Drive — [Fox Sports (Hinchcliffe)](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach); hairpin "now Turn 11, not Turn 10" — search snippet of IMSA/NBC content, snippet only
- Start/finish and lap end: fastest section is Shoreline Drive; drivers "brake hard into Turn 1" — [NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)
- T1: heavy braking, curb-strike opportunity, "overtaking opportunity where a race can be won and lost", bunching at starts/restarts. Best pass is set up by the run out of T11 along Shoreline Drive (push-to-pass use in IndyCar) — [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach); [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)
- T2-T3: the "dolphin fountain section" (scenic, relatively slow); Fox/Hinchcliffe calls it "the 2-3 complex", unlike other corners on the calendar — [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/); [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach)
- T4: "as soon as you head into Turn 4, the track starts to fall down quite a bit" (drop can wash out the front, push to exit wall) — [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach)
- T5: uphill braking zone, second curb-strike opportunity ("Turn 1 and Turn 5") — [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach)
- T6: track "goes downhill aggressively on the entry", climbs mid-corner; NBC calls T6 the braking zone at the end of the Shoreline Drive descent — [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach); [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)
- T7/T8 area: after T6 a short straight past The Pike restaurant/retail complex leads to "a sharp right turn" onto Seaside Way (NBC does not number it; by context this is the T7/T8 region, and it is a RIGHT per NBC — note this conflicts with the user's T7 left / T8 right only if NBC's unnumbered corner is T7; unresolved). Track "falling away again on the entry to the next corner, Turn 8" — [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/); [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach)
- T8 has a tyre wall at its exit ("Watch the tire wall at the exit of Turn 8") — search snippet (LMU guide), snippet only
- Seaside Way back straight runs under a pedestrian bridge linked to the Convention Center, ending in T9 — [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)
- T9: 90-degree right at the end of the back straight; "second-best opportunity for passing" — search snippet of IMSA/racer content, snippet only
- T10: sweeping left (~135 degrees per snippet) in front of grandstands, flowing straight into braking for the hairpin (T11, hard right) — [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/) (unnumbered there); 135-degree figure and "hard right hairpin" from snippet only
- Drivers launch from T11 "past the pit entrance" and onto Shoreline Drive — [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)
- Street names appearing in sources: Shoreline Drive (start/finish, T11 exit to T1), Seaside Way (back straight), Pine Avenue (longer straight on 2000+ layout), Ocean Boulevard — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach_Street_Circuit). No source mapped Aquarium Way or Pine Avenue to specific turn numbers.
- Older layouts: 1992-98 had 9 turns, 1999 13 turns, 2000-present 11 turns (Wikipedia gives no hairpin numbers by year) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach_Street_Circuit). Same 1.968 mi length since 2000 — [RacingCircuits.info](https://www.racingcircuits.info/north-america/usa/long-beach.html)

### Inferences
- NBC's piece skips numbers 7 and 10 and describes "a sharp right" past The Pike; the 2000-present layout (identical length since 2000) means a 10-turn versus 11-turn numbering difference in older maps is a numbering change, not a layout change (inference; no source states why).
- NBC's "clockwise" phrasing (not quoted above because unreliable) conflicts with the user's right-heavy T2-T5 sequence; treat as unreliable.
- T2-T3 being a combined "complex" with T1 left, T2-T5 right is consistent with user telemetry but is unverified.

## Pit lane, start/finish, timing lines

### Takeaway
Pits are on Shoreline Drive beside the traditional start line (since 1983); entry is after T11, exit near T1. Pit lane length, speed limit and sector lines were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Pits and paddock moved to Shoreline Drive, adjacent to the traditional start line, in 1983 — [RacingCircuits.info](https://www.racingcircuits.info/north-america/usa/long-beach.html)
- Cars leave T11 "past the pit entrance" — [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)
- Pit exit is near T1 and the red exit line cannot be crossed to extend the track — Le Mans Ultimate guide, search snippet only (sim, not official)
- The Formula E 2015-16 layout used the Indy pit lane exit — [RacingCircuits.info](https://www.racingcircuits.info/north-america/usa/long-beach.html)

### Inferences
- Start/finish and pit lane both lie on Shoreline Drive between T11 and T1 (follows from the above).

### Gaps
- Pit lane speed limit and length, sector/timing lines, exact S/F position: not found (the 2025 IMSA schedule PDF appeared in search but was not read).

## Overtaking and multi-class traffic

### Takeaway
T1 (end of Shoreline Drive) is the main pass; T9 (end of Seaside Way) is second best; the T10-T11 hairpin is a traffic choke point.

### Cited Findings
- T1 best place; run out of T11 sets it up — [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach); [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)
- T9 second-best pass — snippet only
- A late-race traffic jam at the hairpin "dramatically altered the IMSA race" the previous year — [NBC LA](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/sports/grand-prix-long-beach-street-course-map/3673379/)

### Inferences
- Hard-to-pass: the T2-T8 section (narrow, walls, tight); faster GTP traffic lapping GTD likely loses most time there (inference, no source).

### Gaps
- No class-specific (GTP/LMP2/GTD) overtaking source found.

## Elevation, surface, kerbs

### Takeaway
Real elevation changes exist around T4-T8; surface and kerb data not found.

### Cited Findings
- Track falls at T4, rises under braking for T5, drops on entry to T6 and climbs mid-corner, falls away on entry to T8 — [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach)
- Concrete walls close to the line — [RaceSchool](https://www.raceschool.com/tracks/long-beach-street-circuit/)
- Curb-strike opportunities at T1 and T5 — [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-analysis-james-hinchcliffe-long-beach)
