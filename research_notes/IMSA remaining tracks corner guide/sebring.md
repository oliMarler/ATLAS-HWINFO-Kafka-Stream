# Sebring International Raceway (International layout) corner guide

Research basis: ~10 tool calls. Official sebringraceway.com, IMSA track map PDF images and the McNish IMSA lap (403) could not be read. Several details are therefore single-source or unverified; see Gaps.

## What are the official numbers and names of all corners, and which way does each turn?

### Takeaway
The circuit has 17 turns. Named corners (6, 8/9 chicane, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17, 13, 5, 7) are well corroborated by two independent sources. Directions are fully listed by only one source (Racecontrol, a Le Mans Ultimate guide), so treat them as medium confidence. Turn 3 is named Kristensen Corner. Turns 1, 2, 4, 9 and 12 have no confirmed name.

### Cited Findings
- Current layout is the 8th variation, 1999-present, 3.740 mi (6.019 km), 17 turns. Body text gives 3.741 mi (6.021 km). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebring_International_Raceway)
- Official pit-timing-era name lists: Gurney Bend = T6, Fangio Chicane = between T8 and T9, Cunningham Corner = T10, Collier Curve = T11, Bishop Curve = T14, Gendebien Curve = T15, Le Mans Curve = T16, Sunset Bend = T17, Ulmann Straight = long straight after T16 to the stands, "Flying Fortress" straight between T13 and T14. — [24h-lemans.com](https://24h-lemans.com/en/news/12-hours-of-sebring-legendary-race-track-6374)
- Turn 3 named "Kristensen Corner" for Tom Kristensen, announced at the Sebring Hall of Fame luncheon (article dated 2016-03-18 per fetch; a search summary claimed 2026, which conflicts and looks wrong). Article also lists honorees Cunningham, Collier, Gurney, Gendebien, Bishop, Ulmann without numbers. — [Sportscar365](https://sportscar365.com/imsa/sebrings-turn-3-officially-named-kristensen-corner/)
- Turn 5 = "Carousel", long left, late apex, leads into a 3,050 ft flat section; T7 = "Hairpin"; T8 "Fangio Chicane"; T13 "Tower Turn" (right-angle, strong braking); T14 "Bishop Bend"; T15 "Gendebien Bend"; T16 "Le Mans Curve" (about 90 degrees) onto the 3,082 ft Ulmann Straight; T17 wide, decreasing radius, about 180 degrees, concrete walls. Article dated April 2017 and notes the track had since been revised. — [Yahoo Autos track clinic](https://autos.yahoo.com/track-clinic-everything-know-master-152100390.html)
- Racecontrol (Le Mans Ultimate, i.e. a sim guide, numbering matches the real track):

- NASA Speed: no braking needed at T4, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12 (inconsistent on 12) and 14; T3 brake at "3" board; T10 brake between "4" and "3" boards; T15 entry-speed corner; T17 brake at start of last blue barrier. — [NASA Speed](https://nasaspeed.news/featured/one-lap-around-sebring-international-raceway/)
- Layout history: Hairpin removed 1997 and replaced by a "safety pin" in the 1996-98 layout; Webster Turn replaced by Green Park Chicane in the 1967-82 layout (both are historic, not the current layout). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebring_International_Raceway)
- One history says "Big Bend" was renamed Gurney Bend in 1996 (secondhand via search summary; not verified). — [24h-lemans.com](https://24h-lemans.com/en/news/12-hours-of-sebring-legendary-race-track-6374)

### Inferences
- "Ulmann Straight" is the T16 to T17/start-finish straight (3,082 ft per Yahoo, older article) and "Cunningham Straight" was not found in any source; do not use it as a name.
- The Racecontrol directions (T1 left, T17 right) agree with the Yahoo and NASA descriptions of T1 (wall on right of approach implies left turn), so they are probably reliable. The T7 direction is the one conflict.
- For corner callouts, use "Fangio Chicane" for T8 and T9 jointly, "Sunset" for T17, "Hairpin" for T7, "Tower" for T13.

### Gaps
- Names for T1, T2, T4, T9, T12: none found. "Warehouse" and "Sunset/Bridge" as a separate corner: no source found; "Sunset Bend" is T17 only.
- Hairpin (T7) direction is disputed; verify against a track map.
- Distances from the start/finish line in metres: no source gives them. Only lengths of straights in feet from the 2017 Yahoo article (3,050 ft after T5; 3,082 ft Ulmann Straight).
- Whether "Gurney Bend" is T6 (24h-lemans.com) versus a different turn: one history conflicts, per the search summary.

## Where are the pit entry and exit, and what is the pit lane speed limit?

### Takeaway
Pit speed limit is 60 km/h (about 37 mph) in the 2025 IMSA briefing. Pit exit merges into Turn 1 from the east end of the pit structure. Pit entry is after Turn 17 (inferred); no pit lane length found.

### Cited Findings
- 60 km/h limit begins at red pylons at pit entry and ends at green pylons at pit exit. — [IMSA 2025 Mustang Challenge briefing](https://imsa.results.alkamelcloud.com/Results_NoticeBoard/25_2025/06_Sebring%20International%20Raceway/09_Briefing%20Photos/05_MC%20DRIVER%20BRIEFING.pdf)
- Exiting cars stay two wheels right of the yellow merge line until it ends and "merge safely into Turn 1." — same briefing
- Care Center sits adjacent to pit lane on the eastern end, closest to Pit Out. — same briefing
- Restart Acceleration Zone is Turn 17; a local yellow light is at Turn 17. — same briefing
- A 2026 briefing exists with the same routing but no speed figure was captured. — [2026 briefing](https://imsa.results.alkamelcloud.com/Results_NoticeBoard/26-2026/06_Sebring%20International%20Raceway/09_Briefing%20Photos/05_MC%20DRIVER%20BRIEFING.pdf)

### Inferences
- Pit exit is just before T1 and pit entry is on the T17 / start-finish straight side (pit wall is the braking reference for T1 per Racecontrol, so the pit lane runs along the front straight). The briefing is for Mustang Challenge; IMSA WeatherTech limit is probably the same but not verified.

### Gaps
- Pit lane length, exact pit-entry position relative to T17 apex, and the IMSA WeatherTech-specific limit: not found (briefing map images unreadable).
- Sector and timing-line positions: not found.

## Where are the best overtaking places for multi-class endurance racing?

### Takeaway
Little authoritative guidance was found. Racecontrol identifies two spots; race reports show Turn 1 and the Turn 3 hairpin as contested points in traffic.

### Cited Findings
- Overtaking spots: T13 exit onto the straight into T15, and a good T16 exit to set up a move into T17. — [Racecontrol](https://news.racecontrol.gg/?p=865)
- 2023 12 Hours: GTP cars hit GT traffic into T1; an inside move at T3 sent two cars into the grass. On a restart a wide line into T1 let a rival pull alongside. — [Supercars.net 2023 recap](https://www.supercars.net/blog/sebring-delivers-drama-2023-12-hours-of-sebring-race-recap), [Yahoo Autos](https://autos.yahoo.com/cadillac-control-first-quarter-sebring-171049494.html)
- Long straights after T5 (3,050 ft flat) and T16 (Ulmann Straight, 3,082 ft) are slipstream/drag zones (older article). — [Yahoo Autos track clinic](https://autos.yahoo.com/track-clinic-everything-know-master-152100390.html)

### Inferences
- Best braking-zone passes are probably T1, T3 (Kristensen hairpin), T7 (Hairpin), T10 and T17; hard-to-pass sections are the flat middle (T8-T9 chicane, T11-T12, T14-T15). These are inferences, not sourced statements.

### Gaps
- No driver-sourced IMSA GTP/LMP2/GTD overtaking guide found; the McNish IMSA article returned 403.

## Surface notes (bumpy concrete/airfield)

### Takeaway
Sebring is notoriously bumpy; mixed concrete slabs and asphalt. Worst spots named: T10 exit, both ends of T13, T17, and the T7 braking zone.

### Cited Findings
- T10 exit, both ends of T13 and T17 especially bumpy. — [Racecontrol](https://news.racecontrol.gg/?p=865)
- About 0.9 mile of original coarse concrete and rough transitions remained (2017); T7 braking zone has a pavement undulation (later resurfaced per NASA Speed). — [Yahoo Autos](https://autos.yahoo.com/track-clinic-everything-know-master-152100390.html), [NASA Speed](https://nasaspeed.news/featured/one-lap-around-sebring-international-raceway/)
- WWII-era concrete runway slabs mixed with asphalt cause seam bumps in long corners. — [Box This Lap](https://boxthislap.org/project-motor-racing-presents-sebring-international-raceway/)

### Inferences
- The 2017 concrete figure may be out of date after resurfacing.

### Gaps
- No up-to-date surface map.

## Does iRacing's numbering match the real one, and how does the iRacing version differ?

### Takeaway
Not verified. iRacing's Sebring was confirmed only as a 17-turn full circuit of about 3.7 miles and a Club configuration of 11 turns / 1.75 mi (2013 article). Another sim (Le Mans Ultimate) uses the real numbering.

### Cited Findings
- iRacing described the full circuit as "3.7 miles ... 17 turns" and a Club layout of 11 turns / 1.75 mi (2013). — [iRacing](https://www.iracing.com/down-on-the-runway/)
- A 2023 iRacing event report refers to the "Hairpin". — via search summary, not independently fetched.

### Inferences
- A 17-turn count suggests iRacing uses the real 1-17 numbering, but this is an inference.

### Gaps
- The layout name "Sebring International Raceway - International", iRacing's own corner labels, pit speed limit and any differences from the real track: not found (TrackTitan guide returned 403). Verify in-sim via the telemetry track-config name and lap-distance markers.
